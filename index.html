Silhouette Records is an independent record label founded in 2026 by singer-songwriter Lyla Vox.

Built for artists with something real to say. We exist to discover, develop, and share music driven by genuine emotion, fearless creativity, and a point of view that refuses to blend in. Operating where music, art, fashion, and underground culture meet, Silhouette is a home for the voices shaping what comes next.
